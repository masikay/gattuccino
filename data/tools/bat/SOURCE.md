# Catppuccin for bat

The files in `themes/` are the themes of the official port,
<https://github.com/catppuccin/bat>, unchanged, at commit
`6810349b28055dce54076712fc05fc68da4b8ec0` (2025-06-29): one for each flavor,
`Catppuccin FLAVOR.tmTheme`. They are under the MIT license in `LICENSE`,
Copyright (c) 2021 Catppuccin.

The port has no accent, and a theme of bat has no place for one: gattuccino
copies the one of the flavor in use as `gattuccino.tmTheme`, under a first line
of its own that says so, and tells bat to use it by that name.
