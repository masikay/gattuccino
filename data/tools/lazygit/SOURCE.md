# Catppuccin for lazygit

The files in `themes/` are the mergeable themes of the official port,
<https://github.com/catppuccin/lazygit> (its `themes-mergable`), unchanged, at
commit `1a4b26cbc2b5be05cc5494d0d7f764120a2cf86d` (2026-10-10): one for each
flavor and accent, `FLAVOR/ACCENT.yml`. They are under the MIT license in
`LICENSE`, Copyright (c) 2021 Catppuccin.

The port has the accent: the colors of the border of the active panel and of
the cherry-picked commits are the accent's. gattuccino writes the file of the
flavor and the accent in use, under a line that says whose it is.
