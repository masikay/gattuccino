# Catppuccin for GNOME Text Editor

The files in `themes/` are the style schemes of the official port,
<https://github.com/catppuccin/gnome-text-editor>, unchanged, at commit
`fc7d4f039e35b276750cd7ac4b5947281567343f` (2025-06-23): one for each flavor,
`catppuccin-FLAVOR.xml`. They are under the MIT license in `LICENSE`,
Copyright (c) 2021 Catppuccin.

The port has no accent. gattuccino writes two schemes from them, `gattuccino`
(Latte, the light one) and `gattuccino-dark` (the dark flavor), each naming the
other as its variant, and gives the cursor, where the port has rosewater, and the
number of the current line, where it has lavender, the accent in use.
