# Catppuccin for eza

The files in `themes/` are the themes of the official port,
<https://github.com/catppuccin/eza>, unchanged, at commit
`70f805f6cc27fa5b91750b75afb4296a0ec7fec9` (2025-09-03). One for each flavor and
accent, named `catppuccin-FLAVOR-ACCENT.yml`. They are under the MIT license in
`LICENSE`, Copyright (c) 2021 Catppuccin.

gattuccino copies the one of the flavor and accent in use into eza's
`theme.yml`, under a first line of its own that says so.
