# Catppuccin for btop

The files in `themes/` are the themes of the official port,
<https://github.com/catppuccin/btop>, unchanged, at commit
`f437574b600f1c6d932627050b15ff5153b58fa3` (2024-09-23): one for each flavor,
`catppuccin_FLAVOR.theme`. They are under the MIT license in `LICENSE`,
Copyright (c) 2021 Catppuccin.

The port has no accent. gattuccino copies the one of the flavor in use and puts
the accent in two places, `hi_fg` (the keys that are highlighted) and
`selected_fg` (the selected line), where the port has blue, and says so in the
first lines of the file it writes.
