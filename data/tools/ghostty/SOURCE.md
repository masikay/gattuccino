# Catppuccin for Ghostty

The files in `themes/` are the themes of the official port,
<https://github.com/catppuccin/ghostty>, unchanged, at commit
`92b40c1137b647bdc21f999c098d19d3f59a0227` (2026-11-14): one for each flavor,
`catppuccin-FLAVOR.conf`. They are under the MIT license in `LICENSE`,
Copyright (c) 2021 Catppuccin.

The port has no accent. gattuccino writes the one of Latte as `gattuccino-light`
and the one of the dark flavor as `gattuccino-dark`, and gives the `cursor-color`,
where the port has rosewater, the accent in use.
